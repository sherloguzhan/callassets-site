The platform

One operational record for the whole portfolio.

Asset owners and operators use CallAssets to replace spreadsheets and disconnected portals. Every number traces back to a site, an interval and a source, with role-based access and a full audit trail.

Portfolio analytics

Historical production and revenue across countries and sites, by day, week, month or year, with net and gross revenue composition.

Production data

24 and 96-interval production entry, grid connectors, reviewed XLSX transfer and data-quality coverage checks per site and day.

O&M & availability

Outage and availability logging with categories, multi-day entry and audit-ready exports for operators and contract reviews.

PVsyst baselines

Monthly PVsyst expectations with 25-year production, performance-ratio and revenue projections compared against actuals and irradiance.

Financial operations

Revenue formulas with validity periods, expenses, invoices and payment status, so the asset P&L stays tied to metered production.

Reporting

Site and portfolio reports with performance indicators, batch downloads and exports for investors, lenders and asset managers.

Market intelligence

Production is only half the story. Price is the other half.

We connect asset data to European power markets through ENTSO-E, so owners see what each megawatt-hour was actually worth and where forecasts missed.
